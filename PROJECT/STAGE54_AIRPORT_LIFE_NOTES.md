# Stage 54 airport passengers and staff

The terminal opens with 50 passengers and 13 staff. Travelers start at the entrance, check-in counters, security lanes, café tables, baggage reclaim and gate seating. The same model and voice identity follow each traveler through the terminal. Travelers with rolling cases, hand luggage and backpacks use the existing character renderer and carry three-dimensional luggage.

Passengers reserve seats while they wait for their gate to call boarding. A reservation remains with a seated passenger until they have stood up and cleared the chair. Each staffed queue admits one person at a time from its approach. The line advances in order as the person at its head completes check-in, screening or boarding. Passengers give one another room and move at walking speed along paths authored around the terminal furniture. The café has its own seating visits before screening.

The simulated board shows two rows per gate. A 254-second local schedule staggers the two gates and advances through check-in, boarding, final call and departure. A gate admits its assigned passengers during boarding and final call. The board's boarded count updates when a passenger reaches the concealed end of the boarding path and leaves view. Travelers leaving baggage reclaim use the distinct Arrivals doors. Recycled passengers return from the concealed curbside approach when that origin is out of view and clear for another traveler.

Terminal clocks and movement run near the player during active play. The camera submits no more than 32 terminal characters per frame, using the existing detailed character path. Airport conversations preserve the speaker's place in line or their seat, and nearby ambient reactions require line of sight. Restart rebuilds the local actor pool and schedule from the same identities. The schedule and crowd operate independently of the existing parked aircraft, transport bookings and balances.

Run `node QA/stage54_airport_life.cjs` from the integrated game checkout. The test uses the actual Game through a CPU harness and checks movement, boarding at both gates, seat reservations, queue order, dialogue hold, local activation, detailed character submissions, timetable phases and restart. Over 658.59 local seconds, the test recorded 44 check-ins, 53 screenings, 33 completed boardings, 36 recycled passengers and 35 new arrivals. Boarding, recycling and new arrivals continued during the final interval. The largest visible movement in a single step was 0.131 m. On-device visuals, GPU performance and playback remain unverified by this test.
